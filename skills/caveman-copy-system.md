# Caveman Copy System

The Caveman Copy system turns product features into clear reasons to buy, then
supports those claims with real product evidence rather than decorative filler.

## The six coordinated skills

- `caveman-copy`: the core messaging method.
- `caveman-product-showcase`: orchestrates an opening product card and its four strongest feature cards.
- `caveman-website-copy`: applies the method across complete websites and landing pages.
- `caveman-showcase-website`: builds the interactive category, product, and stacked-card showcase experience.
- `product-visual-proof`: investigates the product and creates truthful, message-bearing supporting visuals.
- `wordsmithing-skill`: sharpens the approved message without changing its meaning.
